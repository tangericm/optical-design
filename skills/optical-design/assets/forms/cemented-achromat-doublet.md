# Cemented achromat doublet

50 mm EFL, f/5, N-BK7 crown cemented to N-SF5 flint, one cemented interface, 486.1 /
587.6 / 656.3 nm. Built from the thin-lens power-split equation, rescaled to the real
thick-lens EFL, then polished on-axis with `LeastSquares` against EFL and
combined-wavelength RMS spot. On-axis RMS spot is 5.3 um against a 3.3 um Airy radius
at 587.6 nm — a couple of waves of residual spherical, typical of a catalog achromat.

Good for narrow-field color-corrected relays: laser coupling, finder optics, a single
element in a longer chain. Past about f/4, spherical grows faster than two glasses can
bend away; past 3-4 degrees half field, coma takes over and no amount of rebending
fixes both — that's what the triplet and Tessar forms are for.

Aberrations, in the order you hit them pushing this form: spherical, then coma
(field limit), then secondary spectrum (irreducible with two glasses). Release the
front and cemented radii first (bending), the flint back radius second for color
balance, and center thicknesses last — they barely move anything here.

Kidger, *Fundamental Optical Design*, ch. 4; Smith, *Modern Lens Design*, ch. 4.
