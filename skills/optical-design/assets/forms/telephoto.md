# Telephoto

127.0 mm EFL, f/5.6, five elements (N-BK7/N-BASF2 front group, air gap, N-ZK7/N-LAF33/
SF1 rear group), 10 degrees half field, 486.1 / 587.6 / 656.3 nm.
`optiland.samples.objectives.Telephoto` unmodified. Total track is 106.1 mm for a
127.0 mm EFL — telephoto ratio around 0.84, noticeably shorter than a normal lens of
the same focal length, which is the entire reason this form exists. On-axis RMS spot
10.9 um against a 4.0 um Airy radius at f/5.6.

Good for any case where focal length is fixed by the application but physical length
is constrained — long-lens photography, compact long-focus relays. A positive front
group and negative rear group separated by most of the system's focal length; the rear
group's divergence compresses the package. Push the ratio much below about 0.7 and the
rear group's aberrations (lateral color, coma) grow faster than a fixed element count
absorbs.

Release the air gap between groups first (telephoto ratio, focus), then the front
group's curvatures (spherical, color), and the rear group last — most sensitive, most
field-dependent.

Kingslake, *A History of the Photographic Lens*, ch. 8; Smith, *Modern Lens Design*,
ch. 8.
